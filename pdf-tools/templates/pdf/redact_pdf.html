How this redaction works: a black box on its own does not redact anything — the text stays selectable underneath. When you apply redactions, every page that contains a redaction is rasterised and the original content is discarded, so the hidden text genuinely cannot be recovered. The trade-off: those pages lose selectable text and get larger. Pages with no redactions are left untouched.
PDF, JPG, PNG, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files are accepted (non-PDFs are converted to PDF first on the server).
